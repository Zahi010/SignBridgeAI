# SignBridge AI: Institutional Briefing & Executive Summary
## Accessible STEM Education for Deaf & Hard-of-Hearing Engineering Students in India

**Audience:** University Vice-Chancellors, Deans of Engineering, Directors of Accessibility Centers, Leadership of Deaf Advocacy Organizations (ISLRTC, NAD India, DEF).  
**Document Purpose:** Executive Briefing on the SignBridge AI Research Pilot.  
**Date:** November 2026.  

---

## 1. The Challenge in Numbers

* **1.8+ Million:** Estimated population of Deaf individuals in India using Indian Sign Language (ISL) as their primary language.
* **< 1%:** Proportion of Deaf students who successfully access technical higher education (B.Tech, B.E., M.Tech).
* **~350:** Total certified educational ISL interpreters nationwide for higher education, almost none with engineering degrees.
* **0:** Prior open-source AI translation platforms adhering to genuine ISL linguistic governance and zero-hallucination policies for STEM education.

---

## 2. What SignBridge AI Is — and What It Is Not

### What It IS:
* An **academic research platform** engineered to translate engineering lecture materials (text, syllabus documents, slides, and real-time speech) into structured ISL intermediate representation and WebGL 3D avatar animation.
* A **governed linguistic repository** where every sign is linked to authentic human recordings from official bodies (e.g., ISLRTC under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment).
* A **community-driven validation tool** where certified Deaf signers and interpreters review and approve technical signs before they are deployed in classrooms.
* An **open-source, privacy-preserving technology** compliant with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016, India's DPDP Act 2023, and international ethics standards.

### What It IS NOT:
* It is **not** an opaque commercial black-box that hallucinates artificial gestures for complex technical words.
* It is **not** an ASL/BSL clone pretending to be Indian Sign Language.
* It does **not** claim that arbitrary technical English can currently be translated with 100% full fluency without human linguistic review.
* It is **not** a replacement for human interpreters; it is an assistive multiplier designed to provide independent study access and structured pedagogical explanations where human interpreters are unavailable.

---

## 3. Four Core Institutional Pillars

1. **The Zero-Fabrication Guarantee:**
   When a student inputs an engineering term that has not been officially codified and validated by the Deaf community, the system will **never invent a sign**. Instead, it transparently flags the term as unvalidated, breaks down the core scientific concepts using analogies and fingerspelling, and logs the term for expert review.
2. **Deaf Community Centered Governance:**
   All signing data is validated using a 12-criteria phonological rubric (handshape, palm orientation, location, movement, non-manual facial markers, regional variation). Deaf master signers possess full adjudication authority.
3. **Institutional Ethics & Compliance:**
   Equipped with granular informed consent models, participant pseudonymization (HMAC-SHA256), automated Right-to-Erasure workflows, and tamper-evident disaster recovery.
4. **On-Premise University Deployment:**
   Fully containerized using standard Docker architecture. Can run locally on campus servers without transmitting confidential exam questions or student records to external corporate clouds.

---

## 4. How Institutions Can Participate in the Pilot

Institutions are invited to join as **Institutional Research Collaborators** in one or more capacities:
* **Academic Review Partner:** Faculty in computer science, linguistics, or engineering review technical concept taxonomies and pedagogical explanations.
* **Linguistic Validation Partner:** Certified ISL interpreters and Deaf educators review candidate avatar animations through the web-based validation portal.
* **Pilot Host Institution:** Host a controlled, IRB-approved evaluation with enrolled Deaf undergraduate students to evaluate comprehension and usability.

**No financial commitment is required.** The software is completely open-source. For collaborative pilots, grant funding pathways and student honoraria will be mutually coordinated.
