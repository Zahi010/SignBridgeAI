# SignBridge AI — External Blockers & Actionable Roadmap

**Document:** `reports/BLOCKERS_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md`  
**Purpose:** Honest technical disclosure of current external dependencies, data gaps, and the specific roadmap required to transition SignBridge AI from an operational software framework into a certified production ISL translation platform.

## 1. Concrete External Blockers & Evidence

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  Current Reality vs Production Need                                |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Component         | Current Repository State           | Production Requirement                   |
+-------------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Human ISL Videos  | 5 FFmpeg testsrc clips (clock/bars)| Certified Deaf master signer 4K recordings|
| 3D Avatar Mocap   | 0 GLB files (procedural mannequin)| 54-keypoint skeletal mocap (ReadyPlayerMe)|
| Linguistic Review | Test developer accounts (#101, #401| Accredited ISLRTC / Deaf community panels|
| Grammar Engine    | 10-verb regex reordering heuristic | Neural dependency translation (iSign/ISL)|
+-------------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
```

### Blocker 1: Absence of Authentic Human Deaf Signer Video Footage
- **Status:** **`BLOCKED` (Awaiting Data Acquisition)**
- **Audit Reality:** The video files in `data/sign_videos/` are synthetic FFmpeg test patterns. They validate the HTML5 video player, HTTP range streaming, and speed controls, but contain zero human sign language content.
- **External Prerequisite:**
  - Licensing open-access educational dictionary clips from the **ISLRTC 10,000-word STEM dictionary** (published on data.gov.in under Government Open Data License - India).
  - Executing professional multi-angle 60fps green-screen studio recordings with certified Deaf master signers and licensed interpreters.

### Blocker 2: Absence of Validated 54-Keypoint 3D Motion-Capture Assets
- **Status:** **`BLOCKED` (Awaiting Motion Capture Pipeline)**
- **Audit Reality:** `data/avatar_animations/neutral_avatar.glb` does not exist. The Three.js viewer renders procedural box/cylinder meshes with solid block hands and zero individual finger phalanges.
- **External Prerequisite:**
  - Rigged humanoid avatar (Mixamo / ReadyPlayerMe standard) with 21 articulated finger joints per hand and 52 ARKit facial blendshapes.
  - Optical motion-capture tracking using **MediaPipe Holistic** or markerless multicamera computer vision extracting skeletal bone rotation quaternions from authentic signing videos.

### Blocker 3: Formal Deaf Community Accreditation & Peer Review
- **Status:** **`BLOCKED` (Awaiting Institutional Partnership)**
- **Audit Reality:** All reviewer records in SQLite were generated by test scripts using mock identities (`[Local Test Reviewer] Certified Deaf Educator`).
- **External Prerequisite:**
  - Establishing a collaborative Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with **ISLRTC New Delhi** or the **All India Deaf Higher Education Consortium**.
  - Convening a panel of Deaf engineering graduates to audit the candidate phonological parameters and approve regional variations (e.g. Northern vs Southern ISL dialects).

### Blocker 4: Rule-Based Heuristic Reordering vs Continuous Translation
- **Status:** **`PARTIAL`**
- **Audit Reality:** `ISLGrammarTransformer` relies on a lookup table of 10 English action verbs and fixed Topic-Comment regex ordering. It does not perform dependency parsing, spatial locus binding, or classifier predicate modeling.
- **External Prerequisite:**
  - Training or fine-tuning an open-source Transformer architecture (e.g., using the **iSign Benchmark** from Exploration Lab, ACL 2023) on bilingual engineering parallel corpora.

### Phase 1: Strict Zero-Fabrication Compliance (COMPLETED)
- Relabeled all synthetic video clips as `DEMO_ONLY`.
- Relabeled phantom GLB avatar entry as `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
- Enforced video suppression when sentences contain unsupported technical concepts.
- Kept 3D avatar in an attentive listening posture, strictly prohibiting fake sinusoidal arm gestures.

### Phase 2: Ingestion of Authentic Open ISL Dictionary Clips
1. Query the **National Digital Library of India (NDLI)** and **data.gov.in** for open-access ISLRTC video files covering foundational technical concepts (e.g., *Machine*, *Computer*, *Measure*, *Light*).
2. Transcode downloaded footage to standard H.264/AAC MP4 format at 60fps with transparent watermarks and GODL-India attribution metadata.
3. Replace synthetic `testsrc` clips with genuine clips and set status to `VERIFIED`.

### Phase 3: High-Density 54-Keypoint Avatar Motion Capture Pipeline
1. Load a rigged glTF humanoid character (`.glb`) into Three.js with complete bone armature:
   - 3 bones per finger (proximal, intermediate, distal) $\times$ 5 fingers $\times$ 2 hands = 30 hand bones.
   - Arms, shoulders, neck, head, spine bones.
2. Build an offline ingestion tool (`scripts/extract_mocap_mediapipe.py`) that processes 60fps signing videos using MediaPipe Holistic and outputs bone rotation quaternion keyframe tracks (`.json` or `.anim.glb`).
3. Implement ARKit blendshape synchronization:
   - WH-questions $\rightarrow$ `browDownLeft: 0.8`, `browDownRight: 0.8`.
   - Polar questions $\rightarrow$ `browInnerUp: 0.7`.

### Phase 4: Vernacular Expansion (Malayalam & Hindi)
1. **Malayalam ISL Module:**
   - Integrate IndicNLP or MLMorph tokenizer for Malayalam root lemmatization.
   - Map Malayalam agglutinative case endings (`-il`, `-al`, `-kk`) to ISL spatial and instrumental loci.
   - Document regional Kerala Federation of the Deaf (KFD) sign variants.
2. **Hindi ISL Module:**
   - Postposition stripping (*ne*, *ko*, *se*, *ke dwara*).
   - Alignment with ISLRTC Northern standard dialect.
