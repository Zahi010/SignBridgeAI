## 1. Invitation & Research Mission

The SignBridge AI Research Initiative invites qualified Indian Sign Language (ISL) experts, Deaf educators, certified interpreters, and linguistics researchers to participate as external evaluators in our independent linguistic validation study.

SignBridge AI is an open-source assistive educational technology platform engineered to make higher education STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) instruction accessible to Deaf and hard-of-hearing students in India.

We are conducting rigorous, peer-reviewed evaluation of our first 5 core engineering signs (*Algorithm*, *Frequency*, *Current*, *Beam*, *Torque*), retargeted to a 3D humanoid avatar. We operate under a strict **Zero-Fabrication Policy**: no synthetic motion will ever be approved or classified as genuine ISL without independent verification by qualified human experts.

## 4. Evaluation Tasks & Multi-Criteria Rubric

Reviewers evaluate each sign across five core phonological and pedagogical dimensions using a 4-point Likert scale (*Excellent / Completely Natural*, *Acceptable with Minor Flaws*, *Partially Distorted*, *Unacceptable / Non-ISL*):

1. **Handshape Articulation:** Finger extension, curvature, thumb position, and contact points.
2. **Spatial Location:** Position relative to head, chest, neutral signing space, and coordinate alignment.
3. **Movement Trajectory & Dynamics:** Speed, repetition, direction of stroke, and deceleration.
4. **Orientation:** Palm orientation and metacarpal facing direction.
5. **Pedagogical Clarity & Technical Accuracy:** Whether the sign clearly communicates the engineering concept to Deaf learners.

Reviewers may also flag **Critical Errors** (phonological violations that change the meaning) and append timestamped annotations.

## 6. Research Ethics, Privacy & Consent

SignBridge AI adheres strictly to international research ethics standards (Declaration of Helsinki, ICMR Ethical Guidelines):
* **Voluntary Participation:** Participation is entirely voluntary. Reviewers may withdraw at any time without penalty.
* **Pseudonymization:** All evaluation scores, annotations, and disagreement logs are indexed under cryptographic participant IDs. Personal contact details are stored in encrypted, access-restricted databases.
* **Right to Data Erasure:** Reviewers may request deletion of their review data at any time via the automated `/api/v1/community/delete-data` API.
* **No Commercial Data Exploitation:** Evaluation data is exclusively utilized for open-source assistive educational technology development and academic publications.
