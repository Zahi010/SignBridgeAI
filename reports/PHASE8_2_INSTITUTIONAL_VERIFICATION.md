## 1. Classification & Status Taxonomy

Every organization is classified using unambiguous, evidence-backed status codes:

- `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_VERIFIED`: Official government or academic website confirmed live and authoritative.
- `PUBLIC_CONTACT_VERIFIED`: Official public email, phone, or inquiry portal verified directly on official domain.
- `DEPARTMENT_UNCONFIRMED`: Institutional website verified, but specific sign-language research group is inferred or unconfirmed.
- `CONTACT_NOT_FOUND`: Organization exists, but no official public inquiry channel could be verified.
- `POTENTIAL_COLLABORATOR`: Meets eligibility criteria for academic/community outreach; no contact initiated.
- `NOT_RELEVANT`: Evaluated and rejected for lack of alignment with ISL STEM education.

---

## 2. Institutional Collaboration Role Distinctions

To ensure clarity during academic outreach, institutional roles are distinguished into six distinct domains:
1. **Linguistic Expertise:** Formal sign language lexicography, phonology, and grammar validation (ISLRTC, LTRC IIIT-H).
2. **Deaf Community Representation:** Cultural advocacy, Deaf user agency, and rights compliance (NAD India, DEF).
3. **Interpreter Training & Accreditation:** Professional certification standards and interpreter evaluation (RCI, AYJNISHD).
4. **Assistive Technology & Kinematics Research:** Computer vision, 3D graphics, and accessibility HCI (ASSISTECH IIT Delhi, R2D2 IIT Madras).
5. **Higher Education Accessibility & Pilot Hosting:** Direct accommodation and STEM classroom evaluation with DHH students (Ashoka University OLS, NDS).
6. **Clinical Speech & Hearing Science:** Auditory, speech, and communication rehabilitation (AIISH Mysuru, HKIDB Mumbai).
