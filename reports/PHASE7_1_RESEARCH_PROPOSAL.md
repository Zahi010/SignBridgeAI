## 1. Overview & Objective

Phase 7.1 developed a comprehensive institutional research and demonstration documentation suite to transition SignBridge AI from an internal development prototype into a formalized research platform prepared for external academic scrutiny, university research collaborations, and Deaf community evaluation.

---

## 2. Prepared Research Deliverables

Five core documentation packages were authored and situated in the root directory:

1. **`SIGNBRIDGE_RESEARCH_PROPOSAL.md`**:
   - Targeted to university computer science, linguistics, and assistive technology departments.
   - Articulates the systemic accessibility barriers faced by Deaf engineering students in India.
   - Contrasts SignBridge AI's modular architecture against naive LLM/ASL direct string substitutions.
   - Outlines a 12-week collaborative pilot program (Phases A, B, and C) with mutual publication and grant funding frameworks.

2. **`SIGNBRIDGE_TECHNICAL_WHITEPAPER.md`**:
   - Targeted to technical collaborators, computer vision engineers, and systems architects.
   - Formal specification of the NLP Intermediate Representation (IR), Topic-Comment planning, and entity disambiguation.
   - Kinematic pipeline detailing MediaPipe Holistic/Vision landmark extraction, anatomical skeleton normalization, and forward kinematics retargeting onto a Three.js SkinnedMesh rig.
   - Mathematical formulation and empirical proofs of Fleiss' Kappa, Cohen's Kappa, and 95% Wilson Score Confidence Intervals.

3. **`SIGNBRIDGE_INSTITUTIONAL_BRIEF.md`**:
   - Targeted to university leadership (Vice-Chancellors, Deans of Engineering, Directors of Equal Opportunity Cells) and executive leadership of Deaf advocacy bodies (ISLRTC, NAD, DEF).
   - High-level executive synthesis framing the legal mandates under the RPwD Act 2016 and National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.
   - Clear delineation of what SignBridge AI is (an assistive, governed research tool) versus what it is not (an unconstrained commercial generative avatar).

4. **`SIGNBRIDGE_DEMONSTRATION_SCRIPT.md`**:
   - Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for conducting structured 15–20 minute live demonstrations.
   - Scripted walkthrough contrasting the translation of an authenticated concept (*Algorithm*) against the Zero-Fabrication refusal for an uncodified concept (*Backpropagation*).
   - Step-by-step guidance for demonstrating the split-screen synchronized player, millisecond annotations, and inter-rater reliability metrics.

5. **`SIGNBRIDGE_PILOT_METHODOLOGY.md`**:
   - Formal protocol reference (`SB-PILOT-2026-v1.0`) defining hypotheses, inclusion/exclusion criteria for expert reviewers (Cohort A) and Deaf student learners (Cohort B).
   - Standardized 12-factor psychometric and phonological evaluation rubric.
   - Explicit declaration that the pilot is maintained in `PREPARATION` pending formal Institutional Ethics Committee (IRB) clearance.
