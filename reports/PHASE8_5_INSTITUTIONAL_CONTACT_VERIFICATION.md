## 1. Audit Framework & Classification Standard

Each of the 12 organizations was independently audited against authoritative government databases (`.nic.in`, `.gov.in`), university institutional directories (`.ac.in`, `.edu.in`), and certified non-governmental organization portals (`.org.in`).

Statuses assigned:
* **`VERIFIED`:** Official domain confirmed, published public contact email confirmed on official contact/directory page accessed within this audit cycle.
* **`PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`:** Domain verified and department confirmed, but contact relies on central registrar or public web inquiry form rather than a named individual.
* **`UNVERIFIED`:** Domain exists but published email could not be definitively linked to the target department.
* **`OUTDATED`:** Published email is defunct, bounced, or domain changed.
* **`INVALID`:** Fictitious or unverified contact detail.
