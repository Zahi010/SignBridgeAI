Subject: Invitation for Academic Collaboration & Research Review — SignBridge AI (Indian Sign Language STEM Higher Education Platform)

Respected Sir / Madam,

We write to you on behalf of the SignBridge AI research initiative, an open-source academic project dedicated to making higher engineering education accessible for Deaf and hard-of-hearing (DHH) students across India.

As you are well aware, undergraduate engineering students in India face immense cognitive hurdles due to the density of technical English terminology. While Indian Sign Language (ISL) is the primary visual language of thought for millions of Deaf citizens, specialized STEM signs (in disciplines such as Computer Science, Electronics, and Mechanical Engineering) remain severely limited in classroom dissemination, and certified interpreters with engineering expertise are virtually non-existent at scale.

Over the past research phases, our team has built a modular, open-source technical prototype featuring:
1. A technical Natural Language Processing (NLP) pipeline that transforms engineering prose into structured ISL Topic-Comment grammatical sequences;
2. High-fidelity MediaPipe 3D motion capture retargeting from genuine ISL master recordings onto a Three.js WebGL humanoid avatar;
3. An architectural "Zero-Fabrication Safeguard" ensuring that our system strictly refuses to invent artificial signs for uncodified terms, providing structured conceptual breakdowns and fingerspelling instead; and
4. An operational web-based linguistic review portal equipped with a 12-factor phonological rubric (handshape, orientation, location, movement, non-manual markers, regional dialect) and automated inter-rater reliability analysis (Fleiss' Kappa).

We have established an initial benchmark on five candidate engineering signs (Algorithm, Frequency, Current, Beam, and Torque) derived from official ISLRTC recordings under GODL-India. However, in accordance with our scientific ethics charter, we maintain that technical motion capture does not equal linguistic correctness. These candidate signs remain strictly "UNREVIEWED" until qualified human signers and linguists independently evaluate them.

Given [Institution Name]'s renowned leadership in [Linguistic Research / Deaf Education / Assistive Technology / Computational Linguistics], we would be deeply honored to explore potential collaboration with your faculty and researchers. Specifically, we would welcome the opportunity to:
1. Conduct a brief, 15-minute live technical demonstration for your faculty and researchers;
2. Receive your expert guidance on our linguistic evaluation methodology and rubrics;
3. Explore avenues for accredited ISL interpreter and Deaf educator onboarding for our planned multi-center pilot; and
4. Discuss potential joint grant proposals and co-authored academic publications.

We emphasize that this is a non-commercial, open-source research initiative. No financial commitment is sought from your institution, and all intellectual contributions will be formally attributed and credited.

We would be grateful if you could grant us an exploratory virtual or in-person meeting at your convenience. Please find enclosed our comprehensive Technical Whitepaper and One-Page Institutional Brief.

Thank you very much for your time, consideration, and dedication to accessible education.
