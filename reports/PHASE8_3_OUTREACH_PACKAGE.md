## 1. Executive Summary & Educational Mandate

Undergraduate STEM education in India presents an acute accessibility barrier for Deaf and hard-of-hearing (DHH) students. While Indian Sign Language (ISL) is recognized under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016 and the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, engineering curricula remain heavily reliant on spoken and written technical English. Certified educational interpreters possessing specialized domain knowledge in engineering (e.g., Fourier transforms, binary search trees, shear modulus) are practically non-existent.

**SignBridge AI** is an open-source, non-commercial academic research initiative engineered to bridge this gap through:
1. **Linguistically Governed Translation:** A domain-aware NLP engine transforming engineering concepts into ISL Topic-Comment grammatical structures.
2. **Authentic Kinematic Retargeting:** A 3D WebGL humanoid avatar executing verified motion capture from genuine ISL master signers (ISLRTC corpus).
3. **The Zero-Fabrication Safeguard:** An architectural guarantee that the system will **never hallucinate an artificial sign** for an uncodified term, presenting transparent pedagogical explanations and fingerspelling instead.
4. **Independent Institutional Review Infrastructure:** An operational web-based evaluation portal where accredited ISL linguists and Deaf educators evaluate signs across a 12-factor phonological rubric.

We invite your institution to collaborate on our upcoming multi-center research pilot.

## 4. Proposed Institutional Collaboration Structure

### Work Package 1: Expert Reviewer Onboarding (Weeks 1–3)
- Briefing institutional faculty, certified ISL interpreters, and Deaf educators.
- Reviewer onboarding via single-use invitation tokens and RCI credential verification.
- Reviewers evaluate the 5 baseline candidate signs using the synchronized split-screen portal.

### Work Package 2: Classroom & Usability Pilot (Weeks 4–8)
- Under formal Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) clearance, 15–30 DHH engineering students evaluate avatar comprehension and pedagogical breakdowns during coursework study sessions.
- Administered using video-based ISL informed consent with complete GDPR Article 17 Right-to-Erasure guarantees.

### Work Package 3: Co-Authored Academic Publication (Weeks 9–12)
- Joint synthesis of inter-rater agreement and comprehension metrics.
- Collaborative submission to peer-reviewed accessibility and assistive technology venues (e.g., ACM ASSETS, IEEE Access, Interspeech).
- Release of validated sign motion capture assets under open data licenses.

## 5. Institutional Protections & Collaboration Principles

1. **Zero Financial Obligation:** SignBridge AI is open-source. Institutions incur no software licensing or platform subscription costs.
2. **Student & Signer Honoraria:** Participating Deaf signers and student evaluators will receive fair research honoraria allocated from collaborative grant funding.
3. **Academic Attribution:** Partner institutions and participating faculty will be credited as co-authors and principal collaborators on all research publications and datasets.
4. **On-Premise Privacy:** Can be deployed locally within the institution's private server infrastructure to protect student privacy.
