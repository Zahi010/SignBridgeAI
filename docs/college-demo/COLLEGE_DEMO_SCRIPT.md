### [0:00 – 1:00] Slide 1–2: Introduction & The STEM Higher Education Barrier

* **On Screen:** Title Slide / SignBridge AI Landing Page (`http://localhost:5173/`).
* **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Good morning, respected members of the evaluation committee and faculty guides. Today, we present **SignBridge AI**—an open-source, linguistically governed Indian Sign Language translation and 3D humanoid avatar platform engineered specifically for higher education in STEM.*
  >
  > *In India, approximately 18 million Deaf individuals encounter a massive accessibility barrier in engineering education. While primary conversational signs exist, advanced engineering concepts—like **Algorithm**, **Torque**, **Frequency**, and **Deadlock**—have historically lacked accessible representation in classrooms.*
  >
  > *Standard closed-captioning tools fail because Indian Sign Language is not English word-by-word; it has its own distinct syntax, spatial indexing, and Topic-Comment grammar. Furthermore, with fewer than 500 certified educational interpreters nationwide, Deaf engineering students are left without classroom support. SignBridge AI bridges this critical gap."*

### [1:00 – 2:00] Slide 3–4: System Architecture & The Zero-Fabrication Charter

* **On Screen:** Architecture Diagram / Top Navbar highlighting "Zero Fabrication Policy".
* **Presenter Dialogue:**
  > *"Before showing the software, we must highlight our core scientific principle: the **Zero-Fabrication Policy**. In AI sign language research, there is a widespread, harmful practice where engineering teams generate synthetic gestures, label them as sign language without Deaf validation, and claim working translation. SignBridge AI strictly refuses to do this.*
  >
  > *Our platform operates on a **Dual-Track Translation Architecture**:*
  > *1. When an authentic, standardized ISLRTC sign exists, the system presents the genuine master signer video synchronized with our 3D motion-retargeted avatar.*
  > *2. When a technical concept lacks an established single sign, the system **never invents a fake sign**. Instead, it executes an automated **Pedagogical Explanation Card**, decomposing the concept into visual analogies and validated constituent signs.*
  >
  > *Let us now see this in action."*
